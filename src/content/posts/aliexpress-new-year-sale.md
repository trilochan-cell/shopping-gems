## How to save big: coupon stacking explained

1. **Collect early.** Store coupons and platform promo codes appear during warm-up — claim everything, even codes you might not use.
2. **Add to cart early.** Prices often drop automatically at checkout once the main sale goes live.
3. **Stack in this order:** store coupon → platform promo code → coins → payment discount.
4. **Filter smart:** use "Free shipping" and "Top rated" filters to avoid slow or unreliable sellers.
5. **Shop midnight drops.** Super Deals refresh at midnight — the deepest discounts sell out in hours.

> Tip: combine a $3-off-$29 platform coupon with a store coupon on the same order for double savings. This single trick beats most "mega deals" banners.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Checking out during warm-up instead of waiting for the main sale window.
- Ignoring shipping fees — a "deal" with $8 shipping is rarely a deal.
- Buying from sellers with under 95% positive feedback just to save a dollar.
