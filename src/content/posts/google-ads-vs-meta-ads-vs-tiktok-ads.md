For a business to grow, the most important thing is to figure out your audience — and then meet them where they already scroll, search, and shop. Google, Meta, and TikTok all promise the best ROI, but they win in completely different situations. Here is an honest, numbers-first comparison.

## The short answer

| Platform | Superpower | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads | Highest purchase intent | Highest CPCs |
| Meta Ads | Best creative testing | Creative fatigue |
| TikTok Ads | Cheapest reach | Needs native-style video |

- **Selling something people search for?** Start with Google.
- **Selling something people discover?** Start with Meta or TikTok.
- **Small budget, big ambition?** TikTok gives you the most impressions per dollar while you learn.

## Google Ads: capture demand

Google wins when intent already exists — someone typing "buy noise-cancelling earbuds" is halfway to checkout. Search campaigns convert 2–3x better than social on average, but competitive keywords can cost $2–$10+ per click. Use exact-match campaigns for your money pages and Performance Max for discovery.

## Meta Ads: create demand

Facebook and Instagram excel at interest-based discovery and relentless creative testing. Broad audiences + 5–10 creative variants let the algorithm find buyers you didn't know existed. The catch: winning creatives fatigue in 2–4 weeks, so budget for a steady pipeline of new images and UGC-style video.

## TikTok Ads: cheap reach, native or nothing

TikTok offers the lowest CPMs of the three — often under $5 — but polished TV-style ads flop. Shoot lo-fi, hook in the first two seconds, and test Spark Ads on organic posts that already perform. Perfect for impulse-friendly products under $50 — exactly the kind of finds we feature in our [deals hub](/deals).

## A starter budget that works

Start with a **70 / 20 / 10 split**: 70% on the platform matching your demand type, 20% on a second channel for testing, 10% reserved to scale whatever wins. Run for two full weeks, then optimize ruthlessly to ROAS — kill ad sets below your break-even, double down above it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which ad platform has the best ROI?
Google usually wins on ROAS for high-intent products; TikTok wins on cost-per-reach for discovery products. There is no universal winner.

### How much should a beginner spend on ads?
$20–$50 per day per platform for at least 14 days — enough data to judge, small enough to survive being wrong.

### Can I run all three at once?
Yes, but only if you can produce platform-native creative for each. One generic video recycled everywhere underperforms on all three.
