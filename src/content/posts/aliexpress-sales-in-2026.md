## Which sales actually matter?

- **11.11** is the undisputed king — the deepest sitewide coupons of the year. Save big-ticket purchases (phones, tablets, e-bikes) for this one.
- **Anniversary Sale** in March is the best spring event, great for fashion and home refresh hauls.
- **Summer Sale** is ideal for outdoor gear, swimwear, and travel accessories.
- Smaller events (Back to School, Christmas) are best for niche categories rather than general hauls.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the biggest AliExpress sale of the year?
The 11.11 Global Shopping Festival in November, followed closely by the March Anniversary Sale.

### How often does AliExpress have sales?
Roughly every 4–8 weeks, with six major events plus smaller monthly promotions.

### Do prices really drop, or is it marketing?
Core event prices genuinely drop — but some sellers inflate "original" prices. Compare against your wishlist history, not the strikethrough price.
