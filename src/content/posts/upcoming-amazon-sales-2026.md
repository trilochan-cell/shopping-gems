## How to get the lowest price on Amazon

1. **Track prices first.** Use a price-history tracker for 2–3 weeks before any sale so you recognize genuine drops.
2. **Stack the bank offer.** The listed discount plus a 10% card offer plus coupons is where the real savings hide.
3. **Compare with AliExpress.** Accessories and gadgets are often cheaper there — see our [New Year Sale stacking guide](/posts/aliexpress-new-year-sale/) before you check out.
4. **Wait for Lightning Deals** on big-ticket items rather than buying on day one of the sale.
