Private plunge pool or champagne at the executive lounge? **Villas and hotel suites** both promise an upgraded getaway — but they suit different trips, groups, and budgets. Here's how to choose, and how to book either one for less with Expedia Group.

## Villas vs. suites: the honest breakdown

| | Private villa | Hotel suite |
|---|---|---|
| Space | Whole property, pool, kitchen | 1–2 rooms, shared amenities |
| Privacy | Total | Shared pool, lobby, lifts |
| Service | Self-serve / private chef extra | Daily housekeeping, concierge, room service |
| Best for | Groups of 5+, families | Couples, business trips |
| Hidden costs | Cleaning fees, security deposit | Resort fees, parking, breakfast |

**Rule of thumb:** for groups of 5+, villas usually win on price per person. For couples, a suite with breakfast and lounge access often beats a villa once you add cleaning fees.

## When a villa wins

- Multi-family trips where a shared pool and kitchen matter more than room service.
- Longer stays (5+ nights) — per-night villa rates drop sharply.
- Destinations where hotels are overpriced in peak season.

## When a suite wins

- Short city breaks where location beats square footage.
- Trips where you want everything handled: housekeeping, dining, spa, late checkout.
- Loyalty play: suite nights earn hotel points and elite-night credits; most villas earn nothing.

## How to save on either booking

1. **Book refundable first**, then re-check prices weekly — rebook if the rate drops.
2. **Compare member prices.** Expedia member rates routinely beat standard rates by 10–25%.
3. **Bundle flight + stay.** Packages cut the total by up to 20% versus booking separately.
4. **Travel shoulder season.** The same villa or suite can cost half as much two weeks outside peak dates.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are villas cheaper than hotel suites?
For groups, usually yes per person. For two people, suites frequently work out cheaper after villa cleaning fees.

### Should I book flights and hotels together?
Bundling on Expedia Group typically saves 10–20% compared to separate bookings — always price both ways.

### Is travel insurance worth it for villa stays?
Yes — villa cancellation policies are stricter than hotels, so refundable rates or insurance matter more.
